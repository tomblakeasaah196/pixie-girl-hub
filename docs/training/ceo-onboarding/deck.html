03 / 10The First Habit
Pixie Girl Hub
Top-left, always

Know which business you're in

The business chip at the top-left is a switch. Flip it and every list, KPI and total on screen re‑loads for that business. Same screens, different money.

◈ Faitlyn Hair ▾
← click to switch
● Pixie Girl Global
◈ Faitlyn Hair ✓

◆ One rule to remember

If a number looks wrong, check the switcher first. You may simply be reading the other business. Switching is safe — it changes nothing, it just re-scopes the view.

▶ TRY IT NOW
~3 min
1
Find the business chip, top-left of any screen.
2
Switch Pixie Girl Global → Faitlyn Hair.
3
Watch the dashboard totals change. Switch back.

04 / 10Getting Around
Pixie Girl Hub
Never go hunting

Get anywhere in two seconds

The left sidebar groups every module. But the fastest route is the command palette — one box that finds records, jumps to any page or tab, and runs commands.

▸ Run ▸ Operate ▸ Finance ▸ People ▸ Grow ▸ System
⌘+K (Ctrl-K on Windows)

Type a module (“invoicing”), a tab (“chart of accounts”), a record, or a command (“new order”). The floating Praxis button is on every screen too.

▶ TRY IT NOW
~5 min
1
Press ⌘K (or Ctrl-K).
2
Type “invoicing” → Enter.
3
⌘K again → “cash” → open Cash Requests.
