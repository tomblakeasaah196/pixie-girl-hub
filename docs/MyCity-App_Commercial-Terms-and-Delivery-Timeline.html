4 · Terms, Change Control & Ownership

  • Change control. Additional or out-of-scope work is captured in a written Change Report, quoted and approved before it starts, and invoiced separately — it does not consume a milestone.
  • Acceptance. Three working days per milestone, after which it is deemed accepted. Three revision rounds per milestone included; further rounds by Change Report.
  • Not in this phase. Recruitment and assessments (§4–§5), payroll and statutory filings (§6), partnerships CRM (§7), expenses and budget control (§8), the AI assistant (§22), native mobile apps and direct social publishing — each available as a separate phase or Change Report.
  • Tax & invoicing. VAT is not currently chargeable: JBS Praxis is below the ₦50,000,000 registration threshold under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025, and will apply it if that status changes. Our TIN accompanies the first invoice; withholding tax deducted at source is evidenced and treated as part-payment. Invoices are due within seven working days.
  • Ownership & data. On final payment MyCity App Ltd owns the delivered custom application and all MyCity data; JBS Praxis retains its underlying reusable frameworks and tooling. MyCity remains the data controller and JBS Praxis the processor under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.
