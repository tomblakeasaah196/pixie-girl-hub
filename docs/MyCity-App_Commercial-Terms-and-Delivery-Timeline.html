2 · Payment — Released on Demo & Acceptance

₦500,000 on signature · the rest on acceptance. Each milestone is demoed, your team tests it, and the fee is released only when you accept it — no milestone is invoiced before you accept it. Values are weighted by effort and risk (see table), not divided equally.
  • Acceptance. Each milestone is demonstrated and handed over for testing; on written acceptance it is invoiced, due in seven working days. No written defect list within five working days means deemed accepted, protecting the schedule.

4 · Terms, Dependencies & Ownership

  • Change control. Work beyond the Product Requirements (§1–§23) is captured in a written Change Report, quoted and approved first, then invoiced separately.
  • Client inputs. Brand assets, organigram, KPI definitions, salary structure, Excel trackers and HR policies by email in week 1; decisions back within two working days. The clock pauses while an input is outstanding.
  • Hosting excluded. Server, domain, SSL, business email, SMS/WhatsApp credits and gateway charges are not part of the ₦4,000,000 — MyCity provides them or authorises us in writing to source and invoice at cost.
  • Compressed schedule. Eleven workstreams run on overlapping parallel tracks; the permissions and audit foundation is laid at M1, the governance console completes at M9, and the AI assistant (M10) ships behind a feature flag.
  • Ownership & data. On final payment MyCity App Ltd owns the delivered application and all MyCity data; JBS Praxis retains its reusable frameworks. MyCity is the data controller, JBS Praxis the processor under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.
  • Invoicing. Issued on acceptance, due within seven working days; withholding tax deducted at source counts as part-payment. Quotation valid 30 days.
